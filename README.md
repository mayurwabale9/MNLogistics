# 🚚 MNlogistic — Logistics Demand & Supply Analysis

## 📌 Overview

**MNlogistic** is a logistics and supply-chain analytics project focused on understanding **demand, supply, inventory, warehouse, shipping, and customer satisfaction**.

The project uses **MySQL, Power BI, DAX, and data visualization** to convert operational data into strategic business insights.

## 🎯 Objective

> **To analyze demand and supply patterns and develop data-driven strategies to improve inventory management, warehouse utilization, delivery performance, and customer satisfaction.**

## 🔄 Analysis Framework

**Demand → Supply → Gap → Inventory → Warehouse → Delivery → Customer Satisfaction → Action**

## 📊 Key Analysis

* Demand trends by product, category, city, and time
* Demand vs. available stock
* Refill and replenishment requirements
* Inventory risk identification
* Warehouse capacity and utilization
* Shipment and delivery performance
* Customer and product ratings
* Strategic recommendations

## 📈 Power BI Dashboard

The dashboard includes:

* **Executive Overview**
* **Demand Analysis**
* **Supply & Inventory Analysis**
* **Warehouse Strategy**
* **Logistics Performance**
* **Customer Impact**

### Key KPIs

**Total Orders | Total Demand | Revenue | Total Stock | Refill Stock | Demand-Supply Gap | Warehouse Utilization | Delivery Days | Customer Rating**

## 🗄️ Data Model

The Power BI model follows a **Star Schema** with:

**Fact Tables:**
`Fact_Orders` · `Fact_Inventory` · `Fact_Shipping` · `Fact_Transactions`

**Dimension Tables:**
`Dim_Date` · `Dim_Customer` · `Dim_Product` · `Dim_Warehouse`

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **MySQL** — Database
* **Power BI** — Dashboard & Visualization
* **DAX** — KPIs & Calculations
* **Power Query** — Data Transformation
* **Excel** — Data Preparation

## 👨‍💼 Role

**Strategic Consultant**

Focused on demand analysis, supply analysis, inventory strategy, warehouse optimization, logistics performance, and strategic recommendations.

## 🏁 Conclusion

The MNlogistic dashboard transforms logistics data into **actionable business insights**, helping identify supply-demand gaps, inventory risks, warehouse constraints, delivery issues, and customer-impact areas.

> **Goal: Move from reactive logistics management to proactive, data-driven supply-chain decision-making.**
